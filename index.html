About

A small studio in Tbilisi

Tiny Eldritch makes compact, thoughtful mobile games: easy to pick up, hard to put down, and fine for players of every age.

Our games are free to play and supported by ads. No accounts, no logins — just open the game and play.

Support

Found a bug or need help?

Email us and include your device model, OS version and the game version (shown in the game's settings). Screenshots help a lot.

Your progress is stored only on your device, so there is no account to delete. Uninstalling the game removes all of its data. Details are in our Privacy Policy.
