Information we do not collect

Our games do not require an account and do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or location. Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the game.

Advertising

Our games are free and show ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, where you allow it);
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • device and app information such as model, operating system, language and app version;
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped, and diagnostic data such as crash information.

Google processes this data under its own policies. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices and consent

  • If you are 13 or older and in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the game's settings.
  • On iOS, for players 13 or older, the game asks for permission before accessing the advertising identifier. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the device's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children

Our games are made for players of all ages, including children. When the game starts for the first time, it asks for the player's age. If the player is under 13 (or under the age of digital consent where they live):

  • only non-personalised ads are shown, from ad networks certified for children's apps;
  • the advertising identifier is not used for tracking or ad personalisation;
  • we do not knowingly collect any personal information.

We follow the Google Play Families Policy, Apple's App Store guidelines for children, and laws such as the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If you are a parent and believe your child's information has been collected, contact us and we will delete it.

Emails you send us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and we keep them only as long as needed for that.

This website

tinyeldritch.com uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security purposes.
